# Functional requirements

## Employee experience

- Use existing B2C authentication and organisational context.
- Capture destination country, client site, stay dates, adults, and rooms.
- Optionally capture traveller, accessibility, facilities, property type,
  rating, distance, cancellation, meals, and special instructions.
- Search Booking.com inventory through the backend.
- Show property content, room and rate, total and nightly price, taxes, payment
  and cancellation terms, availability indicators, distance, and policy result.
- Filter, sort, compare, and view results on a map.
- Revalidate selected availability, price, and policy.
- Capture justification and acknowledgement when required.
- Submit and track a booking request.
- Access confirmed reservation details.

## Operator experience

- Prioritise requests by status, employee, client, destination, dates, value,
  policy, exception, ageing, and priority.
- Review evidence and compliant alternatives.
- Ask for information, approve, reject, change selection, escalate, or override
  according to permission.
- Preview, book, cancel, add notes or attachments, and resend confirmation.
- Distinguish approval, booking attempt, and confirmed reservation.

## Administration

- Manage effective-dated role policies, destinations, sites, approval rules,
  exception thresholds, and operator access.
- Separate business configuration from endpoints, secrets, environments,
  retry, and timeout controls.
- Retain configuration audit history.
