# Inventory mismatch

## Trigger

Selected price changes or the property or rate becomes unavailable.

## Response

1. Revalidate availability and total price.
2. Preserve the prior offer snapshot and record the change.
3. Re-evaluate corporate policy.
4. Display the new price and terms to the operator or employee.
5. Require renewed approval when the configured materiality threshold is
   exceeded.
6. Suggest compliant alternatives, dates, or radius where possible.
7. Never present the earlier offer as confirmed.
