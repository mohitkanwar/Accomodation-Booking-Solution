# Rooms and rate plans

Room and rate information should capture:

- room name and occupancy;
- number of rooms and guests;
- nightly and total price;
- taxes and mandatory charges;
- currency;
- meal inclusion;
- cancellation terms and deadline;
- payment conditions;
- remaining availability where supplied;
- supplier product or rate identifiers.

Corporate policy may restrict room type, meals, price, cancellation
flexibility, and occupancy. The selected offer is snapshotted at submission and
revalidated before booking.
