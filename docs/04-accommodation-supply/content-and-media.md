# Content and media

Search and detail experiences require supplier-provided property name, images,
location, coordinates, category, facilities, rating, review score, and room and
rate content.

Stable property content may be cached according to Booking.com contractual and
technical rules. Availability, final price, and bookable terms require
short-lived handling and revalidation.

Map-provider licensing and property-content usage rights remain discovery
dependencies.
