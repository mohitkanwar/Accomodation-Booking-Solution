# Pricing and promotions

The UI and policy engine require base or nightly price, total price, taxes,
fees, currency, payment terms, and cancellation cost.

Corporate policy should evaluate both maximum average nightly total and maximum
total booking value. Discovery must define:

- whether caps include taxes and fees;
- average versus highest-night treatment;
- per-room versus per-traveller limits;
- FX source and refresh frequency;
- exception amount or percentage thresholds;
- action when price changes after approval.

Price changes require user or operator visibility and policy re-evaluation.
