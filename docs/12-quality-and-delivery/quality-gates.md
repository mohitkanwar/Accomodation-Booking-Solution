# Quality gates

Before integrated booking:

- Booking.com commercial and technical capability confirmed;
- payment and merchant model approved;
- API version and production rate limits confirmed;
- security and privacy review complete;
- policy calculations and precedence accepted;
- authoritative identity and employee-level source established;
- idempotency and uncertain-outcome recovery tested;
- tenant isolation, accessibility, contract, and performance tests pass;
- operator support and reconciliation procedures are ready.
