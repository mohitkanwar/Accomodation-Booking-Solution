# Security testing

Priority scenarios:

- cross-tenant and object-reference attacks;
- manipulated client, role, policy, and price input;
- operator permission and override boundaries;
- secret and personal-data leakage in frontend assets, logs, errors, and audit;
- duplicate and replayed booking commands;
- insecure support or impersonation access;
- environment credential separation.

Formal security-test standards and tooling are not yet defined.
