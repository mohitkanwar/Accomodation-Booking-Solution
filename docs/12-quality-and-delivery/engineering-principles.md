# Engineering principles

- Server-side policy and security controls.
- Internal supplier abstraction and explicit contracts.
- Idempotent and recoverable external commands.
- Immutable snapshots and audit evidence.
- Tenant-aware code and tests.
- Accessibility by design.
- Graceful supplier degradation.
- Observable correlation across workflows.
- Typed and versioned administrative rules.
- Minimal personal and supplier data retention.
