# Search and discovery

Mandatory search criteria are destination country, client site or office,
check-in, check-out, adults, and rooms.

Optional criteria include traveller, accessibility, facilities, accommodation
type, rating, distance, free cancellation, breakfast, and special instructions.

Results support recommended, price, distance, review score, compliance, and
cancellation sorting, plus filters for policy, price, score, category, type,
distance, meals, cancellation, facilities, accessibility, and supported
preferred or sustainable indicators.

Search calls the backend, which resolves policy, invokes the supplier adapter,
normalises responses, calculates distance, and evaluates compliance.
