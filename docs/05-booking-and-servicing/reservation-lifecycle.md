# Reservation lifecycle

1. Resolve employee entitlement.
2. Search accommodations.
3. Retrieve details where needed.
4. Check availability.
5. Select product or rate.
6. Submit employee request.
7. Review and approve or reject.
8. Revalidate availability.
9. Preview supplier order.
10. Create supplier order.
11. Retrieve order details.
12. Persist confirmation and supplier policies.
13. Notify employee and operator.

Request approval, booking attempt, and booking confirmation are separate
business facts and states.
