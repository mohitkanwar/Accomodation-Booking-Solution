# Vision and objectives

Provide Tetrapak employees with a policy-aware accommodation search and request
experience inside the existing Sodexo B2C application, while giving operators
the control and evidence required to approve and complete reservations safely.

## Objectives

1. Allow eligible employees to search Booking.com accommodation inventory.
2. Resolve entitlements from trusted client, employee-level, and destination
   context.
3. Explain whether each proposal is compliant, compliant with warning, requires
   an exception, is not permitted, or cannot be evaluated.
4. Separate employee request submission from the creation of a real supplier
   reservation.
5. Give operators a governed workflow for clarification, approval, rejection,
   exception handling, revalidation, and booking.
6. Protect Booking.com credentials and isolate the B2C application from
   supplier API changes.
7. Retain an auditable record of policies, prices, decisions, overrides,
   booking attempts, and confirmations.

## Discovery questions

- Who may search, request, approve, and book?
- How are accommodation entitlements calculated?
- When does a request become a real reservation?
- Who pays, and whose traveller and payment details are used?
- How does the Lego integrate with existing B2C authentication,
  administration, and operating capabilities?
