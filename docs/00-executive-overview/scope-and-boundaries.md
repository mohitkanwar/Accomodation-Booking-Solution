# Scope and boundaries

## In scope

### Employee

- Enter destination, office, dates, travellers, rooms, and preferences.
- Search Booking.com inventory and view property, rate, tax, cancellation,
  meal, rating, distance, and compliance information.
- Filter, sort, and view results on a map.
- Select a proposal and submit a booking request.
- View request and reservation status and receive outcomes.

### Operator

- Review employee, stay, policy, selected option, and alternatives.
- Request clarification, approve, reject, or escalate.
- Recheck price and availability.
- Preview and create the real supplier order.
- Record confirmation details and service cancellation or modification
  requests where commercially and technically supported.

### Administration

- Manage clients, employee levels, role mappings, destinations, offices, price
  caps, accommodation rules, approval requirements, operator access, and audit
  history.
- Manage non-secret business integration settings.

## Likely outside the first release

- Flights, rail, ground transport, expenses, advances, and loyalty programmes.
- Complex multi-city, group, and full travel-agency functionality.
- Direct employee payment.
- Automated finance-system reconciliation.
- Multiple accommodation suppliers and negotiated hotel-rate ingestion.
- Traveller-risk management.

## Boundary principle

Employee approval is not a booking confirmation. A reservation exists only
after the supplier returns a successful booking confirmation.
