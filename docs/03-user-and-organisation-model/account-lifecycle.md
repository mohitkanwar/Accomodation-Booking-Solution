# Account lifecycle

Employee and operator status should follow authoritative identity and workforce
sources where possible.

The design must address:

- missing or unmapped employee role;
- employee or operator deactivation;
- operator access revocation;
- policy and booking access after an employee leaves;
- retention of historical actor identity for audit;
- access to active or future bookings by authorised support users.

An employee with no mapped role cannot submit a request and is routed to
support or administration.
