# Vendor management

Booking.com management must cover affiliate ownership, enabled capabilities,
API versions, credentials, quota, service expectations, content rights,
commission, payment, support, disputes, traveller contact, security,
termination, and migration.

Map, messaging, payment, FX, and future finance vendors require equivalent
commercial, security, privacy, operational, and exit assessment when selected.
