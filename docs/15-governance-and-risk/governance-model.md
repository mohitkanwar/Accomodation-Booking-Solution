# Governance model

Governance spans Sodexo platform ownership, Tetrapak policy ownership,
Booking.com commercial and technical partnership, operator service ownership,
finance and procurement, and security and data protection.

Required forums and controls:

- Phase 0 commercial and technical gate;
- product and policy approval;
- architecture and security review;
- payment and finance approval;
- supplier onboarding and certification;
- operational readiness review;
- change and incident review;
- maintained decision, risk, assumption, issue, and dependency registers.

Formal forum membership and cadence are not yet defined.
