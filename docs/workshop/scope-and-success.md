# Scope and Success

## Proposed in scope

**Employee:** authenticated access, destination and date selection, Booking.com
search, policy indicators, list/map results, proposal selection, request
submission, status, and confirmation details.

**Operator:** request queue, policy review, clarification, approval or
rejection, live price and availability recheck, booking, confirmation capture,
and supported cancellation.

**Administrator:** clients, employee levels, role mappings, destinations,
policy caps, approval settings, operator access, integration settings, and
configuration audit.

## Proposed outside the first release

Flights, rail and ground transport; expenses and travel advances; loyalty;
multi-city and group travel; traveller-risk management; direct employee
payment; automated finance reconciliation; and multi-supplier negotiated rates.

| Operations | How quickly must requests be processed? |  |
| Booking | What confirmation and failure rates are acceptable? |  |
| Financial | What savings or leakage reduction is expected? |  |

## Decisions required

- Confirm the first-release boundary.
- Define measurable business and user outcomes.
- Identify non-negotiable dates, countries, and user groups.
- Agree what evidence is required before expanding beyond the MVP.
