# Executive Alignment

## Current understanding

Tetrapak needs an accommodation-request and booking capability embedded in the
existing Sodexo B2C application. Employees should discover policy-relevant
Booking.com inventory and submit a request. An operator should review the
request and, where appropriate, create the real reservation.

The main complexity is not the React interface. It is the combination of
Booking.com commercial eligibility, payment ownership, policy interpretation,
price volatility, operator workflow, and recovery from uncertain bookings.

## Outcomes to align

- Give employees a simple, trusted accommodation journey.
- Apply corporate travel policy consistently and transparently.
- Give operators enough information and control to complete bookings safely.
- Maintain an auditable record of policy, approval, booking, and exception
  decisions.
- Integrate without exposing supplier credentials or tightly coupling the B2C
  platform to one supplier API.

## Five framing questions

1. Who may search, request, approve, and book?
2. How is accommodation entitlement calculated?
3. When does a request become a real reservation?
4. Who pays, and whose identity and payment details are used?
5. How does the capability fit the existing B2C platform and operating model?
