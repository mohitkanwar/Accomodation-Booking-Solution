# Runbooks

Initial runbook subjects:

- Booking.com outage or degraded search.
- Expired supplier credentials.
- Booking timeout and uncertain outcome.
- Duplicate action prevention.
- Price or availability change.
- Cancellation failure.
- Missing employee role or destination policy.
- Reconciliation backlog.
- Notification failure.
- Cross-tenant or personal-data incident.

Operational playbooks are scaffolded separately.
