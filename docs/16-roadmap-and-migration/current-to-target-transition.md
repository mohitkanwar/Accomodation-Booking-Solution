# Current-to-target transition

1. Confirm the commercial and operating model.
2. Validate supplier capabilities and B2C Lego integration.
3. Establish trusted employee role data and initial Tetrapak policy.
4. Deliver search, policy evaluation, request, and operator queue with manual
   external completion.
5. Add idempotent integrated booking and reconciliation.
6. Add supported cancellation, modification, finance integration, analytics,
   and supplier optimisation.

This sequence keeps the highest-risk payment and booking operations outside the
first validation increment.
