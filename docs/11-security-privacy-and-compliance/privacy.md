# Privacy

Potential personal data includes employee and traveller identity, work contact
details, stay dates, destinations, property location, phone, special requests,
accessibility or dietary requirements, and payment information.

Discovery must define lawful purpose, collection minimisation, retention,
access, deletion, residency, international transfer, data-subject rights,
support access, masking, and whether sensitive optional requirements are
collected.

A data-protection review is a Phase 0 deliverable.
