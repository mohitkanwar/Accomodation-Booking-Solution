# Payment flows

Candidate questions:

- Does Booking.com, the property, Sodexo, or Tetrapak collect payment?
- Is a corporate card, employee virtual card, prepayment, or pay-at-property
  model used?
- May the operator enter payment details?
- Can card data be transmitted through the contracted API capability?
- How are cancellation fees, refunds, disputes, and failed bookings handled?

No payment flow should be implemented until commercial, legal, security, and
finance owners approve the model.
