# Reconciliation

Integrated booking requires reconciliation for timeouts and uncertain external
outcomes before an operator retries.

A reconciliation record should correlate internal request and attempt
identifiers with supplier references, payment status, amounts, currency,
timestamps, and known supplier state.

Automated finance-system reconciliation is outside the first release; detailed
bank, card, invoice, commission, and supplier settlement matching remains
undefined.
