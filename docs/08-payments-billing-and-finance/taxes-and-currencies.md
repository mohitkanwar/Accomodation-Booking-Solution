# Taxes and currencies

Policy and display require clear separation of base rate, mandatory taxes,
fees, nightly total, and booking total.

Open decisions:

- whether policy caps include taxes and fees;
- source and approval of FX rates;
- conversion timestamp and refresh frequency;
- rounding rules;
- policy currency versus supplier currency;
- VAT and GST identity requirements.

The applied exchange rate and source must be retained with the policy
evaluation.
