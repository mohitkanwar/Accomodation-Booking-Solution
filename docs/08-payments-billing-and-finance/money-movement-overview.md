# Money movement overview

The money-movement model is a discovery gate and has not been agreed.

It must identify:

- merchant of record;
- payer and payment instrument owner;
- supplier or property payee;
- timing of authorisation, capture, charge, refund, and settlement;
- commission recipient;
- invoice owner;
- VAT or GST data requirements;
- reconciliation references and accountable teams.
