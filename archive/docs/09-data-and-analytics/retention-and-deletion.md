# Retention and deletion

Booking history must be retained for authorised support and audit, including
where an employee leaves the company.

The exact retention period, legal basis, deletion process, anonymisation,
residency, international transfer, and data-subject request handling are not
yet defined.

Retention rules should distinguish identity references, requests, bookings,
supplier snapshots, audit events, attachments, notifications, logs, and
payment-related records.
