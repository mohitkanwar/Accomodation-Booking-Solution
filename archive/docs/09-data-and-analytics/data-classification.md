# Data classification

Potential personal or sensitive information includes employee and traveller
name, work email, phone, dates, destination, property location, special
requests, accessibility or dietary requirements, and payment information.

Classification must drive:

- access and masking;
- encryption;
- log redaction;
- support visibility;
- retention and deletion;
- data residency and transfer;
- incident response;
- whether optional sensitive requirements may be collected.

Supplier credentials are secrets and must never be treated as ordinary
configuration data.
