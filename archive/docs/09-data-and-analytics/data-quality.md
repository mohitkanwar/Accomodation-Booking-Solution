# Data quality

Critical quality controls:

- valid employee-to-client and role mapping;
- complete destination coordinates and currency;
- non-overlapping effective policy versions;
- explicit treatment of missing policy;
- validated price, tax, and currency fields;
- current supplier identifiers and terms;
- immutable request-time snapshots;
- booking-reference and status consistency;
- tenant scope on every record;
- redaction of sensitive fields in logs and audit.

Unreliable role data is a major risk because it directly affects entitlement.
