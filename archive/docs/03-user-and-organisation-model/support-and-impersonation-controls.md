# Support and impersonation controls

Support users require object-level authorisation and tenant-scoped access to
historical and active requests.

Any impersonation or act-on-behalf-of capability must:

- be explicitly authorised;
- display the acting and represented identities;
- prevent cross-tenant access;
- create immutable audit events;
- restrict booking, payment, policy override, and cancellation actions;
- mask personal and payment data according to role.

The discovery material does not confirm that impersonation is required.
