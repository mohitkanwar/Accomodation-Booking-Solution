# Partner onboarding

Booking.com onboarding must confirm:

- affiliate eligibility and account ownership;
- permitted employee-only use case;
- enabled search, availability, booking, retrieval, cancellation, and payment
  capabilities;
- approved API version;
- sandbox and production credentials;
- production rate limits;
- content caching and attribution rules;
- payment and merchant responsibilities;
- support, dispute, refund, and traveller-contact responsibilities;
- commissioning and reporting arrangements.

This onboarding is a Phase 0 gate.
