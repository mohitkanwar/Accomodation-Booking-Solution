# Observability

Observability must connect employee request, operator action, internal booking
attempt, Booking.com call, notification, and reconciliation using correlation
identifiers.

Priority signals include search latency and errors, policy evaluation, supplier
429 and timeout, circuit state, booking outcome, uncertain transactions,
operator queue ageing, outbox backlog, and credential failure.
