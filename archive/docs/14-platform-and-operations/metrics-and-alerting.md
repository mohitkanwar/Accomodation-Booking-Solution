# Metrics and alerting

Candidate metrics:

- supplier request volume, latency, error, timeout, and rate-limit usage;
- cache hit and stale-data rate;
- search, request, and operator queue latency;
- policy-result distribution;
- request ageing and exception volume;
- booking success, failure, uncertain, and duplicate-prevention outcomes;
- cancellation outcomes;
- outbox and notification failures;
- reconciliation backlog;
- expired or rejected credentials.

Alert thresholds and on-call ownership remain undefined.
