# Business capabilities

| Capability group | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and eligibility | Authenticate, resolve tenant and role, determine eligibility |
| Destination management | Maintain countries, cities, offices, coordinates, radius, and currencies |
| Policy management | Configure levels, caps, categories, distance, meals, cancellation, approval, and exceptions |
| Accommodation discovery | Search, enrich, normalise, filter, sort, map, and compare properties and rates |
| Compliance | Resolve precedence, evaluate offers, explain results, and preserve snapshots |
| Request management | Draft, submit, clarify, approve, reject, escalate, and track |
| Supplier booking | Revalidate, preview, create, retrieve, cancel, and reconcile |
| Operator servicing | Queue, notes, alternatives, attachments, confirmation, and resend |
| Notifications | Email, push, or B2C inbox notifications |
| Audit and governance | Immutable decision and configuration evidence |
| Administration | Manage clients, roles, destinations, policies, operators, and safe integration settings |
