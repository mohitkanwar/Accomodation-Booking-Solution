# Business model

The solution is an employee accommodation request-and-booking capability
provided through Sodexo's B2C platform for client organisations, initially
Tetrapak.

Employees discover options, but operators control the supplier reservation.
Travel policy is configured per client and employee level, with destination
overrides.

The following commercial elements are unresolved:

- Booking.com affiliate owner and contractual entity;
- commission recipient, if applicable;
- merchant of record;
- payment instrument and settlement model;
- support, dispute, no-show, and refund ownership;
- availability of corporate or third-party inventory rates.

These are discovery gates because they determine whether the proposed in-app
booking flow can operate.
