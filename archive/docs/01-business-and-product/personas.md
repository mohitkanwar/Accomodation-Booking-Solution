# Personas

## Employee requester

Wants to find an acceptable property quickly, understand why an option is or is
not allowed, submit a complete request, and see a trustworthy status.

## Traveller

Needs accurate reservation, cancellation, payment, location, and support
details. The traveller may not be the requester.

## Operator

Needs a prioritised work queue, complete evidence, compliant alternatives,
price and availability revalidation, safe idempotent booking actions, and clear
failure states.

## Operator Supervisor

Needs exception authority, escalation visibility, ageing and workload insight,
and controls for high-value or uncertain cases.

## Client Administrator

Needs effective-dated, validated configuration for roles, destinations,
entitlements, approvals, and exceptions, with change history.

## Platform Administrator

Needs environment-specific integration controls, feature toggles, secure secret
references, auditability, and operational visibility.
