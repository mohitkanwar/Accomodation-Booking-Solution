# Identity and access management

The backend receives trusted employee subject, tenant, role, authentication
level, and locale claims.

Access is permission-based for search, own-request management, all-request
review, policy override, booking, cancellation, policy administration, and
integration administration.

Role level for travel entitlement is not itself proof of application
permission. Operator, supervisor, client administrator, and platform
administrator duties should remain separable.
