# Caching strategy

Candidate cacheable data:

- destinations and offices;
- effective policy and role reference data with explicit invalidation;
- stable property content where supplier terms permit;
- country, currency, category, and facility reference data.

Final availability and bookable price should not be cached beyond a very short
period. Selected offers remain provisional and require revalidation.
