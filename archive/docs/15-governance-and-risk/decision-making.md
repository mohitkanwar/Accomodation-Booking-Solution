# Decision making

Key unresolved decisions:

## Business

Requester versus traveller, delegated requests, out-of-policy submission,
exception authority, mandatory approval, final property selection, weekends,
guests, rooms, contractors, employee cancellation, no-shows, and disputes.

## Payments

Payer, corporate or virtual card, pay-at-property, operator card handling,
prepayment, refunds, invoice ownership, tax details, and reconciliation.

## Policy

Tax-inclusive caps, room or traveller basis, average or nightly calculation,
FX source, exception threshold, preferred hotels, category, cancellation, and
override authority.

## Integration

Approved Booking.com version, booking capability, production quota, webhooks or
polling, caching rights, required terms, support ownership, and redirect
fallback.

Decisions should be recorded as ADRs or business decision records with owner,
date, rationale, consequences, and revisit conditions.
