# Development lifecycle

Delivery begins with a commercial and technical validation phase, followed by
search-and-request MVP, integrated booking, and post-booking optimisation.

The Phase 0 technical spike should validate supplier authentication, sandbox
capabilities, mappings, price and availability behaviour, booking recovery,
and the B2C Lego integration contract before committing to full scope.
