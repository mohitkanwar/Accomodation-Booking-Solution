# Policies and restrictions

Supplier terms and corporate policy are both presented for a proposal.

Relevant restrictions include:

- cancellation deadline and fee;
- free-cancellation preference or requirement;
- prepayment or payment-at-property terms;
- meal inclusion;
- occupancy and room type;
- property category;
- advance booking;
- maximum stay;
- weekend justification;
- maximum distance from office;
- taxes and mandatory charges.

Supplier terms are snapshotted with the selected offer and confirmed again at
booking.
