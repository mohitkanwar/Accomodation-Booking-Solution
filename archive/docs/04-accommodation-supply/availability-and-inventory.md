# Availability and inventory

Search results and selected offers are provisional. Inventory and pricing can
change between search, availability, preview, approval, and order creation.

Required controls:

- search properties, then check availability for a selected property;
- revalidate before request submission;
- revalidate during operator approval;
- revalidate immediately before order creation;
- avoid caching final availability or bookable price beyond a very short
  period;
- show alternatives when a property becomes unavailable;
- reconcile timeouts before retrying an order.
