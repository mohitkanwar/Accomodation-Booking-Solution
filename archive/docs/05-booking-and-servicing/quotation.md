# Quotation

The employee-facing proposal is not a guaranteed quote. It includes:

- traveller, destination, and dates;
- property, room, occupancy, and rate;
- nightly and total price, currency, taxes, and fees;
- payment and cancellation terms;
- policy result and explanation;
- justification requirement and acknowledgement.

Price and availability are rechecked at selection, submission, approval, and
booking preview. Material price changes trigger policy re-evaluation and may
require renewed approval.
