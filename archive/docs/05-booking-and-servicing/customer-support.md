# Customer support

The support model must define ownership across Sodexo, Tetrapak, Booking.com,
operators, and property providers.

Support users require access to request history, policy and offer snapshots,
operator decisions, booking attempts, supplier references, cancellation terms,
notifications, and audit evidence.

Open responsibilities include no-shows, disputes, traveller-property
messaging, refund follow-up, and support after an employee leaves.
