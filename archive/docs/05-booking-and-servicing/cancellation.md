# Cancellation

Employees may request cancellation; direct employee cancellation remains a
business decision. An authorised operator performs the supplier action.

Before cancellation, show:

- current cancellation policy and deadline;
- expected fee and refund;
- supplier and internal booking references;
- confirmation requirement;
- effect on request and booking status.

Cancellation can succeed, fail, or have an uncertain outcome. All decisions,
supplier responses, and financial effects are audited.
